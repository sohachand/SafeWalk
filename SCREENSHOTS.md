# SafeWalk – Screen Guide

Author: Ojaswi Gondalia (I019, SAP 70122400044)

| # | Screen | What the user does / what it shows |
|---|--------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | Splash | Animated logo with pulsing rings and the tagline "Your silent safety companion"; opens the app. |
| 2 | Onboarding | Short intro pages explaining what SafeWalk does. |
| 3 | Sign-in / Create account | Email sign-up or login with Firebase, or continue without an account. |
| 4 | Permissions setup | Explains and requests Location, Contacts and Microphone access. |
| 5 | Trusted circle setup | Add the people who should be alerted (name, phone, relation). |
| 6 | Home dashboard | Safety score ring, insights, quick actions (Start Walk, Follow, Emergency Help, Risk Lab). |
| 7 | Start Walk | Choose start and destination, sensitivity (Relaxed / Standard / Cautious) and the arrive-by timer. |
| 8 | Active Walk | Live fuzzy risk score and tier (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH) with a "why this score" explanation. |
| 9 | "Are you safe?" prompt | 20-second confirmation when risk rises; no reply escalates to an alert. |
| 10 | Manual SOS | Hold the button for 5 seconds to send an alert immediately. |
| 11 | Alert Sent | Shows the live-share code and the SMS status sent to trusted contacts. |
| 12 | Emergency Help | One-tap Call 112 and national helplines. |
| 13 | Nearby help | Opens maps for police, hospital, pharmacy and petrol pump. |
| 14 | Fake call | Simulated incoming call to leave an uncomfortable situation. |
| 15 | Risk Lab | Sliders for stationary time, deviation and night hours with live fuzzy-set curves. |
| 16 | Follow a Walk | A follower enters the 6-digit code to watch someone's walk live. |
| 17 | Walk history / detail | Past walks with risk timeline chart and GPS trail. |
| 18 | Walk summary | Result after a walk: peak risk, final tier and triggers. |
| 19 | Settings | General settings, risk sensitivity, permission management, profile. |
